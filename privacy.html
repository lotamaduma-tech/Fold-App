What we process and why

  • Account information: email, account ID, optional profile name, authentication/session information and account timestamps. Supabase handles passwords for email sign-in. If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase handle the sign-in and may supply email, name, provider identifier and profile metadata, including an avatar URL. The app uses this to authenticate you and associate your records with your account.
  • Your records: income, expenses, savings allocations, amounts, categories, dates, notes, goal names and targets, and links between goals and savings. These produce the balances, history, search results and summaries you request.
  • Workspaces and preferences: Personal/Business workspace names and types, currency, starting balance, spending cap, onboarding choices and timestamps. These organise your records and settings. Workspaces separate your own records; they are not shared team accounts.
  • Technical information: requests to our hosting and authentication providers expose network details such as IP address, browser/request headers and access times to those providers. Their operational/security logs depend on their configuration. The app has no analytics or advertising SDK and sends no financial records to an analytics service. Development-only error diagnostics include operation/status codes, not record contents.
  • Support and privacy requests: information you choose to send through the contact channel once configured, used to respond, verify identity proportionately and resolve your request. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or unnecessary financial documents.

The app does not ask for bank credentials, payment-card details, identity documents, contacts, precise location or date of birth. Avoid entering other people's identifying information or sensitive information in free-text notes unless necessary and lawful.

Browser storage and cookies

Supabase stores session credentials and account metadata in browser local storage to keep you signed in, and temporary PKCE verifier entries to complete sign-in. Treat a signed-in device as access to your account. NectarSpend stores your selected workspace ID per account and a seven-day install-banner dismissal preference. The PWA stores public app files in Cache Storage. It does not cache financial API responses or persist a financial-record database in local storage. Records are loaded into page memory while you use the app.

The application does not set its own cookies or use optional tracking/advertising cookies. Google sign-in and hosting/provider services may use their own mechanisms outside this code; production configuration needs verification. Necessary storage is explained here rather than presenting a marketing-consent banner. Clearing site data removes local sessions/preferences and cached files, not your server-side account.

Retention and account deletion

Currently, records remain in the active database until you delete them or your account. There is no automatic inactivity-deletion schedule. Deleting a goal keeps associated financial records and removes their goal link. The Delete account control is designed to delete your Supabase account and all associated profiles, workspaces, goals and transactions from the active database after confirmation. It requires the account-deletion server function to be deployed. An unavailable or failed request is not confirmation of deletion.

Backup expiry, provider security-log retention and support-request retention must be set and verified: [RETENTION PERIODS AND BACKUP DELETION SCHEDULE]. Deletion from the active database does not promise immediate removal from backups or legally required logs. Any required retention must be limited, explained and protected. Contact the privacy channel for deletion assistance once that channel is configured.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile name and financial entries, change workspace settings, delete records/goals and log out in the app. Email correction and a full access/export request currently require support. You may request access, correction, erasure, portability, objection or restriction where applicable, and withdraw consent for processing based on consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Some rights depend on legal conditions; the operator must explain any justified refusal or retention. Use the privacy contact and identify the request without sending credentials. Identity checks should be proportionate. There is no automated credit, lending or eligibility decision in this app.

You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) about personal-data processing. Contacting NectarSpend first does not remove that right.

Nigeria data protection and children

NectarSpend intends to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 where applicable. Design principles include collecting only necessary information, using it for stated purposes, accuracy, appropriate security, limited retention, transparency and accountability. This policy is not a certification or assurance of complete compliance.

NectarSpend is intended only for users aged 18 or older for this public launch. Do not create an account if you are under 18. We do not collect date of birth or claim to verify age, and no parental/guardian consent flow is provided. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact the privacy channel once configured so the operator can assess and handle removal appropriately. The 18+ policy requires pre-launch review and does not by itself guarantee compliance. Any future service for minors requires a fresh assessment and appropriate safeguards.

Security and policy updates

Technical safeguards include authenticated access, database row-level ownership controls, scoped workspace queries, escaped user text and approved authentication origins. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are needed; operator access controls and incident procedures must also be maintained. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong password, protect your email account and log out on shared devices.
