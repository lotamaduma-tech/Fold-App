A record-keeping tool

NectarSpend helps you organise manually entered personal and small-business financial activity. It does not hold or custody money, receive deposits, operate bank accounts, move money, process payments, lend money, provide consumer credit or deliver investment services. Money movements you record happen outside NectarSpend.

Calculated figures need checking

A calculated balance is a total derived from your entries, not a bank balance or money available to withdraw from NectarSpend. Incomplete, duplicated, inaccurate or incorrectly dated entries can produce incorrect figures. Check your records and summaries against appropriate independent sources before relying on them.

Recorded savings and savings goals do not create deposits or transfer money. Personal and Business workspaces organise records within the same account; they do not create bank accounts or legal entities.

Business summaries

Business summaries are simplified cash and record-keeping summaries. They are not audited financial statements, professional accounting reports or accounting profit calculations. A simple operating result excludes adjustments and costs the app does not model, including inventory costing and tax calculations. It may not meet statutory, tax or accounting reporting requirements.

No professional advice

NectarSpend does not provide financial, investment, accounting, tax or legal advice. Obtain qualified professional advice when appropriate, including before using these figures for business reporting, tax filings, borrowing or other material decisions.
